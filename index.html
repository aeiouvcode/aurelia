Play your own score

Aurelia performs any written score. Open MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) or MEI, or type a tune in ABC. It does not guess a song from its name: something has to write the notes first.

What you are hearing

The music is read from a written score, never from a MIDI file or a recording. Strings, winds, brass and timpani are real instrument samples from the Versilian VSCO-2 Community Edition, and the piano is from the Versilian Community Sample Library. Both are CC0. The choir is synthesized from glottal pulses and vowel formants, because no clean choir samples are available. It is the weakest part and still sounds artificial. Engraving by Verovio.
