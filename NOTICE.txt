Aurelia - third-party material and provenance
==============================================

SCORES
------
All scores in scores/ were written for Aurelia as MusicXML. None were downloaded as
MIDI, MusicXML or any other machine-readable file.

- Lacrimosa (W. A. Mozart, completed by F. X. Süssmayr), Requiem K. 626. The composition
  is public domain. The encoding was transcribed by hand, bar by bar, from a printed vocal
  score (choir and organ reduction). The orchestral parts are an arrangement derived from
  that reduction (violins from the right hand, viola and violin II from the left hand,
  cello and bass from the pedal line, winds and trombones doubling the choir, trumpets and
  timpani at the forte cadences). The encoding is released under CC0.
- Ode to Joy (L. van Beethoven): public-domain theme. The arrangement and encoding were
  written for Aurelia, CC0.
- Prelude in C major BWV 846 (J. S. Bach): public-domain composition, written out for
  Aurelia from memory. Bars 33-34 are close to the original but may differ in detail. CC0.

SAMPLES (samples/)
------------------
- Strings, woodwinds, brass, timpani and organ: Versilian Studios, VSCO-2 Community
  Edition, https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE - Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal
  (CC0-1.0). Trimmed, resampled to 32 kHz mono, loop points added and packed into one
  MP3 per instrument for Aurelia.
- Piano: Versilian Studios, Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), "Grand Piano,
  Steinway B", https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL - CC0-1.0. Processed the same way.
- Choir: no samples. The voices are synthesized in the browser (glottal-pulse source,
  vowel formant filters, ensemble detune and vibrato). No choir sample set with clean
  licensing could be verified, so none is bundled.
